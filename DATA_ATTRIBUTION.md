# Source attribution

The bundled Wikipedia revisions and derived passages are English Wikipedia content. Article titles/page IDs are in `frozen/selected_articles.csv`; exact revision IDs and timestamps are in `frozen/revision_manifest.csv`. Each revision can be attributed via `https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?oldid=REVISION_ID`; its history identifies contributors. The archives preserve source content hashes and derived extraction provenance.

Wikipedia textual content is generally available under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 and applicable additional terms; see https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Copyrights and each revision's notices. Derived extracts retain those obligations. This file does not assert a new license over third-party material.
